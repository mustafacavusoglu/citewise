# Northwind Labs Benefits Guide

Northwind Labs offers benefits to help you stay healthy, keep learning, and plan for the future. These benefits apply to all full-time employees from the first day of employment, unless a section says otherwise. Part-time employees receive them in proportion to their hours.

## Health Insurance

Northwind Labs offers a medical, dental and vision plan through a national insurer. You choose from two plan levels, Standard and Plus.

- The company pays 90 percent of the monthly premium for employees.
- The company pays 70 percent of the premium for spouses, partners and children.
- The annual deductible is $500 on the Standard plan and $250 on the Plus plan.
- Mental health visits are covered at the same level as other doctor visits, with up to 12 sessions per year without a deductible.

You can enroll within 30 days of starting. After that, you can change your plan once a year during open enrollment in November, or after a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Learning Budget

Every employee receives a learning budget of **$1,500 per year**. It can be used for courses, books, conferences, certifications and workshops that help you in your work or career. You can also spend it on online learning platforms.

- The budget resets on 1 January and does not carry over.
- New employees receive a prorated amount for their first year.
- Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, but the ticket can be paid from your learning budget.
- Pay first and submit the receipt in the expense system, using the rules in the travel and expenses policy. Purchases over $500 need approval from your manager before you buy.

We also give each employee 3 paid "learning days" per year to attend an event or study. These are in addition to vacation days.

## Wellness Stipend

The wellness stipend is **$50 per month**. You can use it for gym membership, fitness classes, sports equipment, meditation apps, or other activities that help your physical or mental health. Submit receipts every quarter. The stipend is taxable income only if your local law requires it. Unused amounts do not carry over.

## Retirement Plan

Northwind Labs offers a retirement savings plan. The company matches 100 percent of your contributions up to **4 percent of your base salary**. For example, if you earn $80,000 and contribute 4 percent ($3,200), the company adds $3,200.

- You may join the plan from your first day.
- Company contributions vest after 1 year of service. That means they become fully yours after you complete 12 months.
- You can change your contribution rate at any time in PeopleHub.

## Other Benefits

- Life insurance equal to 2 times your annual salary, paid by the company.
- Employee assistance program with free, confidential counseling for you and your family, available at any time.
- Commuter benefit: $60 per month toward public transport passes or bicycle costs for people who work at the Harbor Vale office.

## Leave and Time Off

Details on vacation days, sick leave and parental leave are in the leave policy.
