# Northwind Labs Remote Work Policy

Northwind Labs uses a hybrid model. Most people work part of the week at the Harbor Vale office and part from home. This policy explains the rules, the equipment support, and what you must do if you want to work from another country.

## Hybrid Schedule

Employees are expected to be in the Harbor Vale office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the anchor days when most teams meet in person. Your manager may agree a different pattern for your team, as long as the 3-day minimum is kept.

Some roles are fully remote. These are agreed in the offer letter and do not follow the 3-day rule. Fully remote employees visit the office at least twice a year for company meetings, and the company pays for the trip.

## Core Hours

Everyone must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 Harbor Vale time on working days. Meetings should be scheduled inside these hours. Outside core hours you may choose your own schedule, but you must still work your contracted hours and respond to urgent messages within a reasonable time.

## Home-Office Stipend

Northwind Labs supports a comfortable home workspace.

- One-time setup stipend of **$600**, which you can use for a desk, chair, monitor stand, lamp or similar items. You can claim it once, after your first 30 days.
- Monthly allowance of $40 to cover internet and phone costs.

Submit receipts through the expense system, using the rules in the travel and expenses policy. Items bought with the stipend belong to you. Laptops, monitors and other equipment supplied by IT remain company property.

## Working From Another Country

You may work remotely from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**. This is allowed because some people want to visit family or extend a trip, but it has limits.

Rules:

1. Ask your manager and the People Team in writing at least 4 weeks before you leave. Both must approve.
2. The days are counted per calendar year and do not carry over.
3. You may not work from a country where the company has no approval from the Legal team. The People Team keeps a list of countries that are excluded, for example because of sanctions or local employment law.
4. You must keep to your normal core hours, or agree a different arrangement in the approval.
5. You must use the company VPN and never work on public Wi-Fi without it.

Please be aware of the tax and visa risks. Working in another country can create tax duties for you and for the company, and a tourist visa often does not allow work. You are responsible for checking that your visa status allows remote work. The company cannot give personal tax advice. Staying longer than 20 working days is not allowed unless you move permanently, which needs a separate relocation approval from the People Team and the CFO.

## Security at Home

Remote work must follow the IT security policy. Keep your laptop locked when you leave it, do not let other people use it, and keep confidential documents out of shared spaces. If your device is lost or stolen, follow the steps in the IT security policy right away.
