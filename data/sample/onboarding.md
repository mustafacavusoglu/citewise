# Northwind Labs Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This guide describes what happens in your first week and the goals we expect for your first 90 days. Your manager and the People Team will help you at every step. The IT helpdesk at it-help@northwindlabs.example can solve any equipment problem.

## Before You Start

The People Team sends you a welcome email 1 week before your start date. The email includes your contract, a first-day schedule and the address of the Harbor Vale office. Your laptop is shipped, or prepared at the office, at least 3 days before you start.

## First-Week Schedule

**Day 1 (Monday)**
- 09:30 Welcome with the People Team and a tour of the office.
- 11:00 Pick up your laptop and set up accounts with IT. You will set up MFA, and you must read the IT security policy.
- 13:00 Lunch with your manager and your buddy.
- 15:00 Meet your team.

**Day 2**
- Security training (90 minutes) and a review of the code of conduct.
- Set up your development environment with your buddy.

**Day 3**
- Product overview from the Head of Product.
- Shadow a teammate during a customer call or a code review.

**Day 4**
- Learn the release process and the on-call basics.
- Your first small task, such as fixing a documentation error.

**Day 5**
- Friday check-in with your manager (30 minutes) to talk about the week and ask any question.

## Buddy System

Each new employee gets a buddy for their first 90 days. A buddy is a colleague from a different team who has worked here for at least 12 months. Your buddy answers everyday questions, introduces you to people, and meets you once a week for 30 minutes. Buddies are volunteers and receive a $50 learning-budget bonus for each person they support.

## Equipment

New employees receive:

- A laptop (14-inch or 16-inch, your choice)
- One external monitor
- A keyboard, a mouse and a headset
- Access to the company password manager and the main collaboration tools

If you work from home, you can also claim the $600 home-office stipend after 30 days, as described in the remote work policy. Report broken or missing equipment to the helpdesk within 1 working day.

## 30/60/90-Day Goals

Your manager sets goals together with you in the first week and reviews them at each milestone.

- **By day 30:** Complete all required training, meet every member of your team, ship one small change to production, and understand how your team measures success.
- **By day 60:** Own a small project or feature with support, take part in code reviews, and give feedback on this onboarding process.
- **By day 90:** Work independently on regular tasks, join the on-call rotation if your role requires it, and agree a development plan for the next 6 months.

At day 90, your manager and the People Team hold a review meeting to confirm that your probation period has been completed successfully. The probation period lasts 3 months.

## Feedback

We want to improve. At day 30 and at day 90, the People Team sends a short survey. Your answers are anonymous when combined with others.
