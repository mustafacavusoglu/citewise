# Northwind Labs Travel and Expenses Policy

This policy explains how to book business travel, what the company pays for, and how to claim your money back. Please spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. It applies to all employees and contractors on company business.

## Booking

Book all flights, trains and hotels through the Voyager travel portal. Booking through the portal means the company can find you quickly if there is an emergency. Use economy class for flights. Business class is allowed only for single flights longer than 8 hours, and it needs approval from the CFO. Book at least 14 days ahead when you can, since last-minute prices are much higher. Rental cars are allowed only when public transport is not practical. Choose a compact car.

## Hotel Limits

The maximum hotel price per night, including taxes, is:

- **Domestic travel: $180 per night.**
- **International travel: $240 per night.**

If the only available hotel is more expensive, for example during a large conference, ask your manager for approval before you book. Without approval, the company pays only up to the limit and you pay the rest. Hotel mini-bar, entertainment and laundry charges for trips shorter than 5 nights are not reimbursed.

## Per-Diem for Meals

When you travel overnight, the company pays a daily amount for meals and small costs. You do not need receipts for the per-diem.

- Domestic: $65 per day.
- International: $85 per day.

On the first and last day of a trip you receive 75 percent of the amount. If a client or the company pays for a meal, reduce the per-diem for that day by $20 for each meal covered.

## Approval Thresholds

Get approval before you book or buy. The approver depends on the total expected cost of the trip or purchase.

| Total cost | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $1,000 | Your manager |
| $1,001 to $5,000 | Your department head |
| More than $5,000 | The CFO |

Approval is recorded in the Voyager portal or in the expense system. Trips to client sites that are already in an approved budget do not need a second approval.

## Receipts and Submitting a Report

Keep a receipt for every expense of $25 or more. A photo of the receipt is enough. Submit your expense report in the expense system within **30 days** of the end of your trip or the date of the purchase. Reports submitted later than 30 days need an explanation and approval from your department head, and may be refused.

## Reimbursement

After your manager approves a complete report, Finance pays it within **10 business days**. The money is sent to the bank account in PeopleHub. If a report is returned with questions, the 10-day period starts again when you resend it.

## What Is Not Reimbursed

- Fines, parking tickets and traffic penalties.
- Alcohol that is not part of a client dinner approved in advance.
- Personal items, travel insurance upgrades and first-class seats.
- Family members' travel costs.

## Questions

Finance can help at finance@northwindlabs.example. For travel emergencies abroad, call the IT and security hotline listed in the IT security policy, and it will connect you with the right person.
