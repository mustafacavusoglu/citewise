# Northwind Labs IT Security Policy

Protecting customer and company data is everyone's job. This policy covers the basic rules for accounts, devices, and data. The IT helpdesk is at it-help@northwindlabs.example, and the security team is at security@northwindlabs.example. The 24-hour IT and security hotline is +1 555 0142.

## Passwords and MFA

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Use the company password manager for all work accounts. Never reuse a work password anywhere else, and never write it on paper or share it in chat.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every company account. Use the authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS codes are not allowed except as a temporary backup, and only for 7 days.
- Passwords change only when there is a reason, such as a suspected leak. Routine forced changes do not apply.

## Device Security

All laptops and phones that access company data must have full-disk encryption turned on. Company laptops are set up with encryption before they reach you. Do not turn it off. Set an automatic screen lock of 5 minutes or less. Install operating system updates within 7 days of release. Only install software from the approved catalog. Personal devices may use email and chat, but they cannot store source code or customer data.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop

If your laptop, phone or security key is lost or stolen, act quickly. Every hour matters.

1. **Report it within 2 hours** of noticing. Call the 24-hour hotline at +1 555 0142 or email it-help@northwindlabs.example. If you cannot reach anyone, email security@northwindlabs.example as well.
2. Tell your manager once IT has been informed.
3. IT will lock your accounts, revoke your sessions and remotely lock or wipe the device. Do not try to do this yourself and do not search for the device in unsafe places.
4. If the device was stolen, file a police report within 24 hours and send the report number to IT.
5. IT will issue a replacement laptop, normally within 2 working days.

You will not be punished for an honest accident, but late reporting is a policy violation. Encrypted laptops that are reported quickly rarely cause a data breach, which is why speed matters.

## Phishing

Be careful with emails or messages that ask for passwords, payments or urgent action. If you receive a suspicious message, click the "Report Phish" button in your email client, or forward the message to phishing@northwindlabs.example. Do not click links or open attachments. If you already clicked a link or entered a password, report it to the hotline within 2 hours, in the same way as a lost device. The security team runs a short phishing test every quarter, and the results are used for training, not for blame.

## Data Classification

All company data belongs to one of four levels:

- **Public**: approved for anyone, such as blog posts.
- **Internal**: everyday company information, such as this handbook. Share only with employees and contractors.
- **Confidential**: customer data, contracts and source code. Share only with people who need it, and only through approved tools.
- **Restricted**: credentials, encryption keys and personal data of employees. Access is logged and needs written approval from the security team.

Never copy Confidential or Restricted data to personal email, personal cloud storage or removable drives. Label documents with their level in the header.

## Leaving the Company

On your last day, IT disables your accounts and you return all company devices within 5 working days.
