# Northwind Labs Code of Conduct

This code describes how we treat each other, our customers and the company. It applies to all employees, contractors and board members, in the office, online, and at events. If something is not covered here, use good judgment and ask your manager or the People Team.

## Expected Behavior

- **Treat people with respect.** Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not allowed, including based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic.
- **Be honest.** Do not mislead customers, colleagues or regulators. Report mistakes quickly, because early reports are easier to fix.
- **Protect information.** Follow the IT security policy and keep customer data confidential, even after you leave the company.
- **Follow the law.** This includes anti-bribery, privacy and export rules.
- **Speak up.** If a meeting has become unfair or unsafe, you are allowed to say so.

## Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests could affect, or seem to affect, your decisions at work. Examples include owning more than 5 percent of a competitor, a supplier or a customer, hiring a relative, or taking a second job in a related field.

You must report any possible conflict to your manager and to ethics@northwindlabs.example within 10 working days of learning about it. Compliance will decide whether the conflict is acceptable, needs conditions, or is not allowed. Outside work is fine, but it must not use company time, equipment or information.

## Gifts and Entertainment

You may accept a gift or hospitality from a customer, supplier or partner only when all of these are true:

- The value is **$50 or less** per gift, and no more than $150 in total from one source in a calendar year.
- It is not cash or a cash equivalent, such as a gift card.
- It is not given while a contract or purchase decision is being negotiated.

Anything above these limits must be declined politely, or reported to Compliance within 5 working days so that it can be returned or donated. You may not give gifts above the same limits to customers, and you may never give anything to a government official without written approval from the Legal team. Modest meals during normal business meetings are fine.

## How to Report Concerns

You can raise a concern in any of these ways:

1. Talk to your manager or your manager's manager.
2. Contact the People Team at people@northwindlabs.example.
3. Email ethics@northwindlabs.example, which is read by the Head of Compliance and the General Counsel.
4. Use the anonymous ethics line at https://ethics.northwindlabs.example, which is run by an outside provider.

Compliance confirms that your report was received within 3 business days. Most investigations finish within 30 days. You will be told the outcome when privacy rules allow it. Reports about the CEO go straight to the chair of the board.

## Non-Retaliation

Northwind Labs does not allow retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or helps with an investigation. Retaliation includes firing, demotion, changed duties, threats or social exclusion. If you believe you are being punished for speaking up, contact ethics@northwindlabs.example immediately. A person who retaliates faces discipline, up to and including dismissal. A report made in good faith is protected even if it later proves to be mistaken.

## Consequences

Breaking this code can lead to discipline, up to and including dismissal and, if needed, legal action.
