# Northwind Labs Leave Policy

Northwind Labs is a software company of about 120 people based in Harbor Vale. We want everyone to rest properly, so this policy sets clear minimums and a simple way to ask for time off. It applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Vacation Days

Vacation days depend on how long you have worked at Northwind Labs. Days are credited on 1 January each year. New employees who start mid-year receive a prorated amount for the rest of that year.

- Less than 3 years of service: **22 vacation days per year**. This is the amount every new employee receives.
- 3 to 5 years of service: 25 vacation days per year.
- 6 or more years of service: 28 vacation days per year.

Vacation days are working days. Weekends and public holidays that fall inside your vacation are not counted.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays each year, plus 2 floating holidays that you can use on any day you choose. The People Team publishes the exact holiday dates every December for the following year. Floating holidays do not carry over and must be used by 31 December.

## Carry-Over

You may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Any carried-over day not used by that date is lost, and no payment is made for it. Days above the 5-day limit expire on 31 December.

## Sick Leave

Every employee has 10 paid sick days per year. You do not need a doctor's note for an absence of up to 3 consecutive working days. For longer absences, send a medical note to the People Team. Please tell your manager as early as you can on the first day you are unwell, ideally before 10:00. Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

If you are ill for more than 10 working days in a year, the People Team will talk with you about extended medical leave. Pay during extended leave follows the benefits plan and local law.

## Parental Leave

Parental leave is available to all parents, including adoptive and foster parents.

- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks at full pay.
- Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks at full pay.

Leave must start within 6 months of the birth or placement. It can be taken in one block or split into two blocks. When you return, you may work a reduced schedule of 80 percent for the first 4 weeks while keeping full pay.

## How to Request Leave

1. Open PeopleHub and choose "Request time off".
2. Pick the leave type and the dates.
3. Your manager approves or declines the request within 3 working days.

Requests of 10 or more consecutive working days need at least 4 weeks' notice and also require approval from your department head. Parental leave should be announced to the People Team at least 8 weeks before the planned start, where possible. Sick leave is not requested in advance. Instead, log it in PeopleHub after you return.

If two people in the same team ask for the same dates, the earlier request is approved first. Managers should avoid leaving a team with less than half of its members present during the last two weeks of December.
